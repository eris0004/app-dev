# My Favorite Series: The Umbrella Academy 🦸‍♂️🦸‍♀️

## Overview
*The Umbrella Academy* is an epic superhero series on Netflix created by Steve Blackman. It follows the dysfunctional Hargreeves family who reunite to save the world from apocalyptic threats.

## My **Favorite Characters** 👑
1: Number Five   
Time-traveling assassin with sarcastic wit. The smartest, deadliest 58-year-old in a 13-year-old body!

2: Klaus Hargreeves   
Chaotic ghost-whisperer & party king. Comedy gold with a heart of gold. Best character arc ever!

3: Diego Hargreeves   
Knife-throwing vigilante with perfect aim. Loyal bad boy who always has your back!

4: Allison Hargreeves   
"I heard a rumor" mind control. Civil rights fighter turned superhero powerhouse!

5: Viktor Hargreeves   
Violin soundwave destruction. Deepest emotional journey of the family
